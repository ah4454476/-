## 🍳 Ingredients
* 500g Chicken breasts (sliced)
* 3 tbsp Butter
* 4 cloves Garlic (minced)
* 1/2 cup Heavy cream
* 1/2 cup Chicken broth
* Salt & Black pepper to taste
* Fresh parsley for garnish

## 🔪 Instructions
1. Season chicken slices with salt and pepper.
2. Heat a pan over medium heat and melt 2 tbsp of butter. Sear chicken for 5-7 minutes per side until golden brown, then set aside.
3. In the same pan, melt remaining butter and sauté minced garlic until fragrant.
4. Pour in chicken broth and heavy cream. Bring to a low simmer.
5. Add chicken back into the sauce and simmer for another 3 minutes. Garnish with parsley and serve warm!
