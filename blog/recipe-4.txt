## 🍳 Ingredients
* 200g Dark chocolate (melted)
* 1/2 cup Unsalted butter (melted)
* 1 cup Granulated sugar
* 2 large Eggs
* 1/2 cup All-purpose flour
* 1/4 cup Cocoa powder
* 1/2 tsp Vanilla extract
* Pinch of salt
