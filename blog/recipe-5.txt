## 🍳 Ingredients
* 2 ripe Avocados (diced)
* 1 cup Cherry tomatoes (halved)
* 1 English cucumber (chopped)
* 1/4 cup Red onion (thinly sliced)
* 1/4 cup Feta cheese (crumbled)
* 2 tbsp Olive oil
* 1 tbsp Fresh lemon juice
* Salt & Black pepper to taste

## 🔪 Instructions
1. In a large bowl, combine chopped cucumber, cherry tomatoes, and red onion.
2. Add diced avocados and crumbled feta cheese gently to avoid crushing the avocados.
3. Whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper in a small cup.
4. Drizzle dressing over the salad, toss lightly, and serve immediately.
