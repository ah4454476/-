## 🍳 Ingredients
* 1 Pizza dough base
* 1/2 cup Tomato pizza sauce
* 150g Fresh Mozzarella cheese (sliced)
* Fresh Basil leaves
* 1 tbsp Extra virgin olive oil
* Pinch of salt

## 🔪 Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to its maximum temperature (220°C / 425°F).
2. Stretch the pizza dough onto a baking tray or pizza stone.
3. Spread tomato sauce evenly across the dough, leaving a thin border around the edge.
4. Place fresh mozzarella slices on top.
5. Bake for 10-12 minutes until crust is golden and cheese is bubbly.
6. Top with fresh basil leaves and drizzle olive oil before serving.
