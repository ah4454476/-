<h3>Ingredients</h3>
    <menu>
	<li>1 cup chicken (boiled)</li>
            <li>1 cup boiled rice</li>
            <li>1 teaspoon chicken powder</li>
            <li>1 cup finely chopped cabbage</li>
            <li>3 eggs (2 beaten)</li>
            <li>½ cup all-purpose flour</li>
            <li>200g cheddar cheese, cut into cubes</li>
            <li>1 cup breadcrumbs</li>
            <li>Salt to taste</li>
            <li>1 tsp ground black pepper</li>
            <li>Chopped fresh cilantro, to taste</li>
            <li>2-3 green chilies</li>
            <li>1 tsp roasted cumin powder</li>
            <li>Oil as needed</li>
   </menu>
    <hr />

<h3>Instructions</h3>
    <ol>
	<li>First, chop the rice and chicken finely.</li>
            <li>Then, add one egg, chicken powder, green cilantro, green chili, salt, black pepper, and cumin, and mix well.</li>
            <li>Transfer this mixture to a bowl, add the cabbage, and mix again.</li>
            <li>Spread it out on a tray, place a cube of cheese in the center, and roll it into a ball.</li>
            <li>Now, in a bowl, mix the flour, black pepper, and salt. Coat the balls in the flour, then dip them in the beaten egg, and finally coat them in breadcrumbs.</li>
            <li>Heat oil in a pan and deep-fry the balls over low heat until golden.</li>
            <li>Delicious, energy-packed rice balls are ready.</li>
            <li>Serve with ketchup or mint chutney.</li>
   </ol>

<h4>For Nutritional And Health Benefits</h4>
<p> This delicious and energy-rich dish can be a nutritionally balanced choice as it contains a good combination of protein, carbohydrates, fiber and healthy minerals. Chicken provides high-quality protein that plays an important role in strengthening the body's muscles, repairing cells and maintaining daily energy. Rice provides the body with instant energy, while cabbage, being rich in fiber, vitamin C and various beneficial nutrients, helps in improving digestion and strengthening the immune system. Eggs are an excellent source of protein, vitamin D and other essential nutrients, while cheese provides the body with calcium and protein, which are essential for strong bones and teeth. Green coriander, green chilies and cumin seeds not only enhance the taste but also help in improving digestion and adding aroma. If this dish is eaten with a fresh salad in adequate quantity, it can be part of a balanced diet and proves to be an excellent choice for providing energy for children, teenagers and busy people. </p>

<h4> For Pro Tips And Substitute Ingredients </h4>
<p> To prepare this dish with the best taste and attractive texture, it is very important to keep a few important things in mind. The rice and chicken mixture should not be too soft or too dry, because the right texture helps in maintaining a beautiful round shape. If the mixture feels too soft, a few fine breadcrumbs can be added to make it firm. It is important to seal the cheese well in the middle so that it does not come out during frying. Keep the oil temperature moderate because too hot oil will quickly darken the outer layer while the inner part will not be fully cooked. Similarly, too cold oil can absorb more and make the dish heavy. To make it more healthy, brown rice can be used instead of white rice, which increases the fiber content. Similarly, fine oat flour or wheat flour can be used in limited quantities instead of regular flour. If you want to avoid deep frying, they can also be prepared in the oven or air fryer with light oil, which reduces fat while maintaining a good degree of crispiness. </p>

<h4> For Serving And Storing </h4>
<p> This delicious dish is best enjoyed warm, with its flavour and soft texture of cheese. It can be served with a fresh vegetable salad, mint sauce, light yoghurt sauce or tomato sauce to further balance the flavours. If it is to be served as a complete meal, a light vegetable soup or a fresh fruit drink is the best choice. It is also very suitable for entertaining guests, children's lunches or light evening snacks. If there is any leftover, let it cool completely first, then store it in an airtight container in the fridge to prevent moisture from entering and spoiling the crispy crust. If stored properly, it can stay good for up to two days. When reheating, it is better to reheat it in the oven, air fryer or in a covered pan over low heat rather than in the microwave, as this way the outside becomes crispy again and the cheese inside remains soft and delicious, while the overall taste feels almost like a freshly prepared dish. </p>
